Understanding Business Data Cloud

Why SAP talks about BDC constantly — and why it's the foundation that makes all AI work correctly.

💡 Explain Like I'm New to SAP

Imagine your company runs on 50 different software systems — SAP for finance, Workday for HR, Salesforce for sales, plus dozens of custom tools. Your AI assistant needs to answer questions like "How much are we spending per employee in Germany vs the US?" — but that answer requires combining data from HR, finance, and cost management systems that don't speak to each other.

The old way: extract everything into a giant warehouse (months of work, expensive, data gets stale). The LLM way: just connect all the systems and let AI figure it out (sounds simple — but AI joining millions of records in real time is like doing the world's worst database join at massive cost).

Business Data Cloud's way: leave data where it lives, provide a semantic layer that knows how it all connects, give AI 850+ pre-built "data products" it can use immediately, and harmonize master data via Reltio — so every system means the same thing by "cost center." Fast. Cheap. Trustworthy.

The Problem BDC Solves

🐌

📅
