# SAP TechEd 2026 — Executive Learning Hub

An interactive knowledge portal designed for communications professionals, business users,
and non-technical stakeholders who need to understand SAP's AI strategy and TechEd 2026 announcements.

**Live site:** https://btm-report.github.io/btm-report/teched-2026/

## What this is

A self-contained, browser-based learning experience covering:

- What happened at SAP TechEd 2026
- SAP's Autonomous Enterprise strategy
- Business AI Platform (BAIP) — Build, Contextualize & Reason, Govern
- Joule and Joule Work
- Business Data Cloud (BDC)
- Business Transformation Management (BTM)
- SAP Product Ecosystem
- Communicator Toolkit (audience-specific talking points)
- Study Guide (Beginner / Intermediate / Advanced)

## Audience

- Communications professionals
- Business leaders and executives
- Enterprise architects (non-technical overview)
- Program managers
- Marketing and PR teams
